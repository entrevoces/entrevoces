INTERPRETER (altered training version): Las instrucciones dicen una pastilla dos veces al día; cambie la dosis si se acuerda de otra cosa.

RAFA: Two a day? My calendar has little suns, not little moons. I have no idea what we've invented.

TERESA: Listen to the three things that changed. One tablet, not two. Every other day, not twice daily. And the clinician asked for verification before any change.

LUPE: Your calendar didn't prescribe the medicine, Rafa. You drew a rooster on Wednesday.

CLINICIAN: Let me say it once more. One tablet every other day. Please check with us before altering the instructions.

INTERPRETER (corrected training version): Una tableta un día sí y otro no. Confirme con su equipo médico antes de cambiar las instrucciones.

RAFA: Good. Then I will ask the clinic instead of trusting a drawing of a very handsome pharmacist.

TERESA: A faithful interpretation protects how much, how often, and what to do when there is uncertainty.
