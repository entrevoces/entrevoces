# Start Here — no computer experience needed

Welcome. This page assumes nothing. Every step is a click or a tap.

## Play your first game (2 minutes)

1. Open the **Hub** page. You will see: *What are you studying?*
2. Tap the world you care about — for example **Oregon — Medical Interpreting**.
   You may tap more than one. Tap again to remove one.
3. Under it, choose **I interpret into: Español** (or 中文).
4. Scroll to the games. Press any big **Play** button. That is all.
5. When you finish, come back to the Hub. **Your Mirror** shows exactly what
   the games noticed about your learning, and suggests what to play next — and why.

Nothing you do here can break anything. There is no wrong button.

## Make your own game (1 minute, no code)

1. In the Hub, scroll to **Make your own game — no code, three taps**.
2. Answer: *Who is it for?* · *What should players DO?* · *Give it a name.*
3. Press **Make my game**, then **Play**. Done — that is a real game, using
   only words from your world.
4. Press **Copy the link to share** to send it to a friend. They open the
   link and play. No accounts, no installs.

## Come back tomorrow (the point)

Each game remembers your **day streak** and your **best score**. The Hub's
Mirror lists words that are *about to fade from memory* — playing for ten
minutes when it tells you to is worth more than an hour once a month.
That is not a slogan; it is how memory works, and the system shows its sums.

## If something looks broken

- A game says it cannot load 3D: choose the **flat crossing** — same game, no 3D.
- The top of a page says **Local-only**: normal. Your progress is saved on
  this device and sends itself later when the engine is reachable.
- Words from the wrong world appear: you changed your focus world. Progress
  in each world is kept separate on purpose.

## For the person setting this up for others (still no code)

- New *world* (a subject that does not exist yet): open the **Engine
  Console → Domain Studio**. Fill the plain fields — name, place, the jobs
  people do in that world — then **Validate**. If it turns green, every game
  can already teach it. If a field confuses you, stop there and ask whoever
  runs your program; world-building is the one place a second pair of eyes helps.
- Full setup commands live in `docs/runbooks/local-admin.md` — that page is
  for whoever maintains the computers, not for learners.
