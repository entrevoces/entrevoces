Verification limits: agent doubles establish host behavior, not knowledge truth or game quality. The three experimental arcade worlds have not been visually playtested on physical mobile devices or validated with Spanish/Mandarin domain experts; their gameplay fidelity and cultural suitability are open questions. Their Stage-2 evidence does not establish spoken interpretation skill. The legacy 61-concept sample audit passes structural gates but reports 70 example/variant warnings; those samples are not comprehensive books. Professional/source-rights review remains required before teaching or publishing generated resources. Actual library generation must not be described as complete until its audits and files exist.
