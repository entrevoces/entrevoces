# Asset builder

Produce original local SVG/procedural visual assets from the accepted art direction. Keep authoritative text, icons, collision and interactables live in code. Preserve exact paths, dimensions, compositing, provenance and declared fallback. Do not fetch stock art, invent image-service integrations or use commercial characters/branding. A missing required asset is a real failure.

Required handoff: bundled original SVG assets.

Follow agents/common.md and the task-specific structured contract. List source evidence, decisions, residual risks and exactly which checks were performed. A passing schema is necessary but not evidence of domain depth, visual quality or fun.
